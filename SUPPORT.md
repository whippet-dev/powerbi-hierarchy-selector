# Support

Multi-Field Selector is maintained by Whippet Dev.

## Getting help

- Review the [README](README.md) for setup, selection behaviour and formatting
  guidance.
- Report defects or request improvements through the
  [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/whippet-dev/powerbi-multi-field-selector/issues).
- For matters that should not be discussed publicly, email
  [whippet.dev@gmail.com](mailto:whippet.dev@gmail.com).

When reporting a problem, include:

- the Multi-Field Selector version;
- the Power BI Desktop or Service version;
- clear reproduction steps and the expected behaviour;
- screenshots or console messages, where useful; and
- a minimal, anonymised example if the problem depends on a particular data
  shape.

Do not share credentials, confidential report files, personal information or
production data in a public issue.

## Support scope

Support covers installation and use of the visual, reproducible defects, and
clarification of documented behaviour. General Power BI administration, data
modelling, report design and bespoke visual development are outside the
standard support scope.

Support is provided on a best-effort basis. No guaranteed response or
resolution time applies unless separately agreed in writing.

## Security reports

Do not disclose a suspected security issue publicly. Email
[whippet.dev@gmail.com](mailto:whippet.dev@gmail.com) with the subject
`Multi-Field Selector security report` and include enough detail to reproduce
the issue safely.
