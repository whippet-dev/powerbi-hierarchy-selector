# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 August 2026

Multi-Field Selector is a Power BI custom visual published by Whippet Dev.

## Data processed by the visual

The visual processes the report data, field metadata, formatting settings and
filter state that Microsoft Power BI supplies to it. This processing is used
only to render the visual and apply the selections requested by the report
user.

The visual does not send report data, personal data or usage data to Whippet
Dev or to any external service. It does not use external web requests,
advertising, analytics, tracking pixels or cookies.

## Stored settings

Formatting settings and the visual's explicit selection state can be persisted
by Power BI as part of the report. This information remains under the control
of the report owner and the Microsoft Power BI environment in which the report
is stored.

Whippet Dev does not receive or retain this information.

## Third-party services

The visual does not connect directly to third-party services. Use of Power BI
itself is governed by the applicable Microsoft terms and privacy statements.

## Support communications

If you contact Whippet Dev for support, the information you choose to provide
will be used only to investigate and respond to your request. Do not include
confidential report data, credentials or personal information in a public
GitHub issue.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the visual's functionality or applicable
requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when a material
change is made.
